Where did you come from?

In Hungary if you move abroad you're not required to give back your residency card. Voting abroad is also done in various embassies around the world, and you're going to vote for the candidates in the same place your residency card shows. The link between voting at foreign embassies and to which constituciency the vote eventually went can be tracked, as this is one of the statistics the National Election Ofice tracks. Now obviously some people voting abroad are just temporary visitors, but - especially near larger embassies - most of the voters will actually be immigrants, who have already moved residency abroad.

This website tries to show where people in each foreign embassies lived originally, effecitvely displaying an emigration map.

Usage

The left side shows the list of embassies. Clicking on any of them will show the number of voters of each county and constituciency on the right side. By default yellow colour mean less, red colour mean more people.

On the bottom left side are the controls. You can choose which year you wish to check here. You can also check comparisons between two years, in which case light blue colour will mean a slight decrease in voter numbers, and darker blue will mean a larger decrease. Yellow and red will remain to be used for the increase of numbers.

Since the number of total voters in each counties / constituciencies are not the same, which can skew the colourings, you can click on the "Linear" button to swap to a more "Proportional" display, in which case counties with less population will be coloured proportionally darker, and counties with more population lighter.

If you're not comfortable with the default colouring scheme to swap to a grayscale colouring mode

You can also swap back from embassies to the global view by clicking on the "Selected city" button at the bottom left.
