Where did you come from?

In Hungary if you move abroad you're not required to give back your residency card. Voting abroad is also done in various embassies around the world, and you're going to vote for the candidates in the same place your residency card shows. The link between voting at foreign embassies and to which constituency the vote eventually went can be tracked, as this is one of the statistics the National Election Ofice tracks. Now obviously some people voting abroad are just temporary visitors, but - especially near larger embassies - most of the voters will actually be immigrants, who have already moved residency abroad.

This website tries to show where people in each foreign embassies lived originally, effecitvely displaying an emigration map.

Usage

The left side shows the list of embassies. Clicking on any of them will show the number of voters of each county and constituency on the right side. By default yellow colour mean less, red colour mean more people.

On the bottom left side are the controls. You can choose which year you wish to check here. You can also check comparisons between two years, in which case light blue colour will mean a slight decrease in voter numbers, and darker blue will mean a larger decrease. Yellow and red will remain to be used for the increase of numbers.

You can also swap back from embassies to the global view by clicking on the "Selected city" button at the bottom left.

Settings

By default the system shows absolute numbers. Each voter is counted as a single voter regardless where they come from.

However the counties and constituencies have different population, therefore large counties, like Budapest would also show up more visibly. If we want to make the display more equal clicking on "Linear" will swap the display to "Proportional". In this mode each county and each constituency will be assumed to have the same amount of voters and the colouring will be done accordingly.

When checking a comparison between two years, by default the difference is going to be shown as absolute voter differences. If we want to know how the voter number has changed relatively to other embassies, you can click on the "Normal" button to swap to "Time-Proportional" representation. For example between 2018 and 2026 the number of voters globally have multiplied by 1.5x. So if at an embassy in 2018 a 100 voters were present we could assume that in 2026 there will be 150 voters. Under "Time-Proportional" representation if only 140 appeared in 2026 we would show it as a decrease of 10 voters. Under "Normal" display we would show it as an increase of 40.

If you're not comfortable with the default colouring scheme to swap to a grayscale colouring mode

Notes

Constituency boundaries have changed in 2026. Budapest lost two, and Pest County gained two constituencies. When doing any comparisons involving the year 2026 these new constituencies will be assumed to have 0 voters in 2022.

Due to the war in Ukraine, the embassy in Kyiv moved to Lviv in 2022, then moved back to Kyiv in 2026. On the map the 2022 data still shows the embassy in Kyiv to make comparisons easier to make.
